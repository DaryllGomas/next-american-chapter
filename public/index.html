The fine print

This is an independent citizen project. Nobody named here has endorsed it, agreed to a role, or been asked yet. Matthew McConaughey's October 2026 video urged people to consider independent candidates. It endorsed no one and wasn't about him running. This page is a hypothetical built on that idea.

How the picks were made. Think of it as a general picking a team for one mission: AI everywhere it helps, cheap and abundant power, healthcare and education people can actually get, lifting the people who've been left behind, and clean, open government. Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini each picked the strongest person for every seat on their own, without seeing each other's answers. They scored speed, AI fluency, results for people at the bottom, a clean record, being ready to serve all of 2028–2032, and trust across the divide. Party, gender, race and "balance" counted for nothing. The AI pick is where they agreed, or the strongest evidence when they split. "Close call" means it was genuinely hard. Think we got one wrong? Vote, write in your own, or open a pull request on GitHub.

How voting works. One vote per seat per browser. Change it anytime, or click your choice again to take it back. Every vote passes a Cloudflare human check, and each network has a daily cap. We don't store IP addresses, only a hash that changes every day. It's not bot-proof. Nothing is. If we see gaming, we'll tighten it and say so.

Write-ins. Anyone can write in their own pick for any seat. Once three different people write in the same name, it shows on that seat's board and others can back it with one tap. Write-ins with strong support get added to the ballot as a top pick. Reasons appear after a quick review for spam, links and personal attacks.
