# Fact-check of `public/slate.json` (v3.0-merit): 2026-10-18

Scope: every bio and evidence line for all 72 candidates in 24 seats, plus X handles for all 72.
`slate.json` was not edited. Lines like "two of three AIs picked him" describe the method and were not checked.

Severity labels used below:
- **Error**: wrong as written.
- **Outdated**: was true, but the person's role has changed.
- **Overstated**: true in substance, but worded more strongly than the sources support.
- **Optional**: accurate, but a more current detail is available.

Caveat: the research session hit its web-search limit partway through. The following people were cleared from Wikipedia and official bios, without a fresh 2025–26 news search:

- Brainard, Hsiang, S. Young
- Mattis, Rosenstein, Campbell, Flournoy, Melroy
- Rus, Torres Small, Wheeler, Regan

- The later scandal checks in the Families, Lived Experience and Council seats

Run one quick news search on each before posting. I also couldn't check whether any X account is active, because x.com can't be fetched. Spot-check each handle on X before tagging.
