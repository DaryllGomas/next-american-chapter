## Why it's built this way (from X's ranking code, xai-org/x-algorithm, weights synced 2026-10-16)

- **Image, not link, in the main post.** Original posts from small accounts get a cold-start boost for about 2 hours.
  Grok reads the image to find non-followers.
- **Link in the reply.** A post with a link X rates "low quality" is hidden from non-followers, and the risk is higher
  when the post also tags accounts that don't follow you. A brand-new workers.dev link has no reputation yet.
- **Exactly two @mentions.** Two or more triggers a Grok spam check, so the post has to read as a genuine message.
- **End on a question.** Replies count about 5x and shares about 20x more than a like.
- **No bait** ("RT if", "tag someone"). That gets a post labeled spam.
