# How the slate was decided

Source thread: ChatGPT conversation "McConaughey Administration Plan" (Daryll's account), 2026-10-18.

1. **Daryll's prompt.** McConaughey as president, Musk as backer, and a team that lifts the "lower K":
   healthcare, education and opportunity, AI, power, space and robotics. Picks loved by left and right and
   chosen for character and track record, not pedigree.
2. **ChatGPT draft 1.** VP Spencer Cox (Polis as the alternative), 13 mission leads, Musk outside government.
3. **Claude's critique.**
   - Skewed: 5 Democratic-affiliated picks to 3 Republican, and mostly academics.
   - Missing seats: housing, Treasury, Labor and rural America.
   - Daryll's own picks were dropped: Diamandis and Wissner-Gross.
   - No lived-experience seat.
   - Proposed pairings: Cass with Autor, Roy with Topol, and Isaacman for space.
   - Fact fixes: the video was on Instagram, Musk has given about $276M to GOP groups, and there's no sign the
     America Party is active.
4. **ChatGPT v2.** 24 seats, and it pushed back: balance means philosophies plus competence, not headcount.
   Musk gets no say over appointments. The lived-experience seat needs real power, not a ceremonial title.
5. **Claude's round 2.**
   - Justice goes to Horowitz instead of Yates.
   - Agriculture goes to Heitkamp.
   - Mostaque and Beshear are dropped as alternates.
6. **Final fix (Claude).** Whitman became the environment primary to even the ledger.
   **Final ledger: 5 L · 5 R · 3 C · 11 Ind.**

Leans are judgment calls about public records, not party registrations.

## Round 3: Grok (2026-10-18)

Grok's verdict: "11 Independent" was a dodge, the policy direction skewed left, and the framing overclaimed what
McConaughey's video said. See [grok-review.md](grok-review.md).

**Labels.** Claude proposed honest two-part labels: lean (L / lean-L / none / lean-R / R) and type
(official / builder / expert), plus a published note on how the labels were assigned. ChatGPT agreed, with one caveat:
expertise isn't a lean, so academics stay "none" unless there's evidence.

**Swaps:**
- AI governance: Thierer (lean-R) as primary, Nelson as alternate.
- Energy: Wright (R) as primary, Shah as alternate.
- Defense: Flournoy (L) as primary, McMaster as alternate.
- Relabels: Pahlka becomes lean-L, Whitman lean-R and Isaacman lean-R.

That overcorrected to 5 left / 8 right, so there were two more changes:
- Environment: McCarthy (L) as primary, a left-leaning check on a right-leaning energy secretary.
- Space: Ellen Ochoa (none) as primary, with Isaacman as alternate because of his SpaceX ties next to Musk.

**Final tally:** 6 left-associated, 6 right-associated, 12 with no known lean. Grok: "Sign off ... No remaining vetoes."

## Round 4: merit only (2026-10-18)

Daryll: "It really doesn't matter who's left, who's right ... The only thing that matters is picking the right person for
each one." That retired the balance-by-label approach from rounds 2 and 3.

**Method:**
- Claude wrote its own picks first ([merit-round-claude-blind.md](merit-round-claude-blind.md)).
- ChatGPT (a temporary chat with no memory) and Grok (a new chat) each got the same blind prompt. The rule: judge on
  record only; ignore party, gender, race and balance; conflicts and age count. See
  [merit-round-grok-blind.md](merit-round-grok-blind.md).
- The AI pick is whoever two or more of the three lists agreed on. Ties went to the strongest concrete evidence.
  Runners-up come from the other lists.

**Notable outcomes:**
- Walsh (Labor) was picked by all three. Daniels (OMB) was the only name on all three lists.
- Daryll's own Diamandis stays the Abundance pick. Wissner-Gross becomes a runner-up to Bengio, who is on two of
  the three lists.

**Sign-off:** ChatGPT reviewed the merged slate (in a fresh chat) and replied "sign off". Grok had hit its weekly
limit and couldn't review it. That limit resets 2026-10-20.

**Each AI's own lean showed up in its blind picks.** ChatGPT leaned toward recent Biden-era officials, and Grok
included sitting Trump officials. Merging three blind lists is what cancels that out.

## Round 5: the mission team (2026-10-18)

Daryll: "If I'm a general, who are the people I want in my troop to accomplish this mission?" Round 4 picked the
strongest résumés. This round picks the people who can carry out the vision.

**Scorecard:** SPEED, AI FLUENCY, LIFTS THE BOTTOM, CLEAN, READY (able to serve all of 2028–2032, no Musk conflicts) and
BRIDGES.

**Blind voters:** Claude, ChatGPT (a temporary chat with no memory) and Gemini. Grok was out until Oct 20, so Gemini
took its place. Gemini's list had a few names that couldn't be verified, so only real people were counted.

**Merge:** a name on two or more lists wins the seat. Splits went to the strongest evidence.

**Biggest changes:**
- Shalanda Young (OMB): Daniels would be 79 in 2028.
- Jon Huntsman (State): ambassador under Obama and Trump.
- Patrick Collison (Abundance): picked over Diamandis, who is now a runner-up.
- Christine Wormuth (Defense): Flournoy fails CLEAN.
- Michael Regan (Environment) and Xochitl Torres Small (Agriculture).
- New builder runners-up: Amanda Renteria, Rachel Carlson, Alex Oshmyansky (Cost Plus Drugs), Daniel Yang (Kaiser AI),
  Melonee Wise, Will Roper and Kathleen Hicks.

### Round 5 decisions (Claude, after the mission fact-check)

Daryll: "Those aren't my decisions. They're yours." The calls, made against the scorecard:
- **Healthcare: Kinsley over McClellan.** McClellan sits on the Johnson & Johnson and Cigna boards, which fails CLEAN
  for the affordability seat.
- **Space: Melroy over Bridenstine.** It was a 2–2 tie. Bridenstine is now CEO of Quantum Space (CLEAN) and was
  confirmed on a party-line vote (BRIDGES).
- **Abundance: Collison stays.** Musk was an early Stripe investor, but it's an unpaid outside advisory seat, and Fast
  Grants is the strongest SPEED evidence of any candidate. Diamandis has a Musk tie too: the XPRIZE Carbon Removal
  prize was funded by Musk's foundation.
- **Burns stays a State runner-up.** The Feb 2026 Epstein-file reports allege no wrongdoing.
- **Swapped out:**
  - Carlson/Romer stepped down as Guild CEO; replaced by Palandjian.
  - Danielle Brian stepped back from POGO; replaced by Rosenstein.
  - Prabhakar was listed twice; Barzilay takes her AI science spot.
  - Henderson has a 2016 ethics censure; replaced by DiCerbo.

### Final review (ChatGPT, fresh temporary chat)

ChatGPT agreed with all four of Claude's calls. Its condition on Collison: public disclosure and recusal. That's now a
rule for every pick, and it's stated on the page. It proposed three vetoes:
- **Accepted: AI science goes from Fei-Fei Li to Kathy Yelick.** AMD agreed in Sept 2026 to buy World Labs, and Li is
  slated to become AMD's chief scientist (READY and CLEAN). Yelick has run Berkeley Lab since July 2026. Li stays a
  runner-up.
- **Not accepted: Energy stays with Jigar Shah, not David Turk.** The DOE inspector general's interim concerns were
  about contractors' conflict controls, and Shah disputed them. No finding against him personally was found. On SPEED,
  which the power build-out needs most, Shah has the strongest record. Turk was added as a runner-up, and the seat is
  marked a close call.
- **Not accepted: Lived experience stays with Donald Whitehead, not Michael Tubbs.** The seat is for someone who has
  lived poverty and homelessness to review policy. Whitehead was homeless for five years and now leads national
  homelessness policy work. Tubbs stays a runner-up.
