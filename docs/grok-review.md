# Grok's review (round 3 reviewer), 2026-10-18

Source: grok.com chat "Third Reviewer Critique of Balanced Ticket Slate". The Grok CLI's usage balance was exhausted,
so this ran in the browser. Summary of the main points:

- **Right-leaning readers will object to** Nelson, Poo, Shah, Brainard and Heitkamp (all L). They'll also say
  Pahlka is labeled C too generously (she was Obama's deputy CTO).
- **Left-leaning readers will object to** Cass, Roy, McMaster, Daniels and Cox (all R). They'll also say Whitman as C
  is generous (a lifelong Republican who joined Forward and endorsed Biden).
- **"11 Independent" is a dodge.** Many of the "Ind" picks are academics or technologists who sit left of center
  culturally. The ledger looks balanced while the substance skews left of center.
- **Swaps it suggested:** Nelson → Thierer (R), Shah → Chris Wright (R), Cass → Julie Su (L),
  McMaster → Flournoy (L).
- **Framing.** McConaughey's video says it isn't about him and endorses no one. Calling the page "built around"
  it overclaims.
- **On X,** expect both sides to dunk on it. Its suggested post:
  "McConaughey: vote independent so both parties have to deal. Hypothetical 2028 team—him president, @elonmusk
  backer (money + megaphone, zero appointments). 24 seats. 5L/5R/3C/11Ind. Thumbs up/down or swap every one. [link]"
- **Design risks:**
  - The newspaper look can read as coastal and elite.
  - The lean squares invite "who assigned these labels?"
  - The niche seat names feel like a think-tank exercise.
- **Gaming.** Cookies, proxies and solver farms beat cookie and IP caps. Its cheapest real fix is "Sign in with X"
  plus a per-account limit, or email verification, plus publishing aggregate counts so manipulation is visible.
