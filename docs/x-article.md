# The Balance Ticket: not left, not right

Matthew McConaughey just posted a video asking a simple question: what if enough independents won seats that
Democrats and Republicans *had* to work together?

I took that seriously, and went one step further. If McConaughey were president, who would actually be on the
team?

Not a celebrity cabinet, and not a donor cabinet. People picked for what they've built, run or lived through:
people who could deliver AI, cheap reliable power, healthcare, education and real opportunity, measured by how
much life improves for the people who've had the least chance to share in it.

## How we built it

I had two AIs argue it out. ChatGPT drafted a team. Claude tore it apart: too many picks from one side, no one on
housing or rural America, and nobody who has actually lived what being "left behind" means. They went back and
forth until they agreed.

The result is 24 seats with a balanced ledger:

**5 left · 5 right · 3 center · 11 independent**

Every pick has its lean labeled, so you can see the balance for yourself instead of taking my word for it.

## Two seats are set

- **President: Matthew McConaughey.** He can talk to everybody, and that's the job.
- **Outside backer: Elon Musk.** Funding, a megaphone and industrial know-how, with **no government role and no
  say over appointments.** No company should write its own rules. Yes, he's put about $276M behind Republicans
  this cycle. This is an invitation to back something different: balance.

## Everything else is yours
